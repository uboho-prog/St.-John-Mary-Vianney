Providing Love, Dignity, and Care to Senior Clergy and Religious

When religious and diocesan clergy reach their final chapters, many uncover a difficult reality: having never married or accumulated personal wealth due to vows of poverty and lifetimes of service, they can easily find themselves without a safety net or family caretakers. St. John Mary Vianney Group provides fully structured housing facilities, clinical healthcare programs, and continuous lay-parishioner companionship so that they can conclude their earthly journeys in safety, prayerful community, and peace.

WHAT WE DO

Our Comprehensive Care Ecosystem

By combining comfortable group homes with a specialized, dedicated team, we fulfill every dimension of our retired priests' needs: material, social, and spiritual.

Fellowship Gatherings

We arrange regular social luncheons, family days, and group travel excursions. These events reconnect retired religious with parish life and parish families, offering them the laughter, appreciation, and human warmth they deeply deserve.

Medical & Health Support

Our volunteers manage physician schedules, provide safe transportation, coordinate rehabilitation, and cover co-pays for critical prescription medications. On-site registered practical nurses visit residential homes multiple times a week.

Spiritual & Pastoral Care

We maintain beautifully ordained sanctuaries inside residences, enabling our priests to host and celebrate Holy Mass together. We honor their spiritual vocation, keeping them integral parts of direct church liturgies and online live chapels.
